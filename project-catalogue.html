About the catalogue

The IPFO Project Catalogue lists infrastructure projects in Indonesia that are open to private investment and partnership, together with how ready each one is. First introduced at the International Conference on Infrastructure (ICI) 2025, it is updated periodically as projects progress.

What each project profile covers

  • Location, scope and the proposed role for private partners
  • Investment scheme (PPP, B2B and others), government support and preparation status
  • Indicative capex, financial metrics and timeline
  • A contact person at the submitting institution

Who it is for

Investors and lenders; developers, operators and advisers looking for partnership opportunities; and the public officials who plan, finance and deliver infrastructure.

Project information is indicative and may change as projects develop. Please confirm the current status with the project owner, or ask IPFO.
