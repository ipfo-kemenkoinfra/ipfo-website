Privacy Notice

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This notice explains, in plain language, what the Infrastructure Project Facilitation Office (IPFO), under the Coordinating Ministry for Infrastructure and Regional Development (Kemenko Infra), does with the information you send us through this website. We handle personal data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022).

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the contact form, we collect:

  • your first and last name and email address;
  • your organization and country, if you provide them;
  • the type of enquiry, the project it is about (if any), and your message;
  • that you agreed to this notice, the date and time, and the page you sent the form from.

When you subscribe to updates, we collect your email address, the date and time, and the page you subscribed from.

Please do not send confidential or commercially sensitive information through the website. We will follow up by email and agree on a suitable channel if more detail is needed.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, facilitate contact with the ministries, agencies or project proposers concerned.
  • To track enquiries internally so that each one is followed up.
  • To send updates you subscribed to. Each update will tell you how to unsubscribe.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website and Project Catalogue are used, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

  • IPFO staff who handle enquiries.
  • Relevant government institutions or project proposers, only when needed to answer or facilitate your enquiry (for example, the contact for a project you asked about).
  • Service providers that run parts of the website for us: Google (form storage in Google Sheets, email notifications, and Google Analytics) and Cloudflare (the Turnstile security check that blocks spam). They may process data outside Indonesia.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 2 years after our last exchange with you, then deleted, unless we must keep them longer under Indonesian archiving rules.
  • Subscriptions: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your email.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to unsubscribe, at any time by emailing sekretariat@ipfo.kemenkoinfra.go.id with the subject "Privacy request". We will reply within a reasonable time and may need to confirm your identity first.
